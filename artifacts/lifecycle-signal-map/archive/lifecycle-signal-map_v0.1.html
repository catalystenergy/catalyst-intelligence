Layer 01
Project Lifecycle
01 ·
Policy & Sector Reform
Govt commits to sector direction. IPP frameworks, utility restructuring. No projects yet — pipeline being created.
02 ·
National Planning & Programme Design
IRPs, electrification masterplans, WASH investment plans. Projects conceptualised and budgeted.
03 ·
Project Identification & Prefeasibility
Specific project scoped. Feasibility TORs drafted. DFI prep grants appear. 12–24 month lead on RFP.
04 ·
Feasibility & Structuring
Detailed studies, technology selection, financing structure. ESMP baselines. Long phase — many consultants.
05 ·
Transaction & Procurement
RFP issued. Bids prepared. Preferred bidder selected. Most visible phase. Most crowded.
06 ·
Financial Close & Construction
Debt and equity assembled. EPC contracted. Limited advisory entry unless lender's/owner's engineer.
07 ·
Operations & Asset Management
Tariff reviews, benchmarking, refinancing, expansion planning. Underserved entry point.
